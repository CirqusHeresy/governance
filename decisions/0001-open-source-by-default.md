# 0001 — Public work is open source by default

- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Scope:** both
- **Status:** adopted (founder decision, before the chambers are constituted)
- **Decided by:** founder

## Context

The Order asks creators and members to trust its mechanisms. Trust in a mechanism nobody can read is faith, and the manifesto asks us to keep consequential mechanisms understandable. At the same time, the Order needs income to sustain its Houses.

## Decision

- The protocol (contracts, specifications) is published under **MIT**.
- Texts, guides and governance documents are published under **CC BY 4.0**.
- Paid services (e.g. Inquisition incident response, Arsenal assays, premium tools) may be built on top of the open protocol and may be closed-source or source-available.
- Nothing that controls members' funds or votes may be closed-source.

## Consequences

- Anyone can fork the protocol. We accept this: *keep the exit real*.
- Revenue comes from services and expertise, not from locking up the core.

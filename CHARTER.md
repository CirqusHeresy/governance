# Charter of the Order — proposed framework

> **Status: proposal.** This text takes effect only when the members adopt it. Until then, decisions are made by the founder and recorded in [`decisions/`](decisions/).

## 1. Purpose

Cirqus Heresy, the Order of Choice, builds institutions for sovereign creators: structures in which people can create, protect their work, find backing and conduct research while remaining its authors.

## 2. Circles of participation

| Circle | Who | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| **Outer Ring** | Seekers — read, question, test | Temple NFT |
| **The Planet** | Members of the two chambers | Seal(s) |
| **The Pole** | The Hexastorm — six coordinating seats | Seat |

This is not a ladder of rank. It is a map of participation, from the outside in.

## 3. Signs and chambers

**Creative Chamber** — Trickster (question), Inquisitor (proof), Oracle (vision), Alchemist (transformation), Architect (construction).
**Leverage Chamber** — Mage (leverage: capital, access, allies).

- Roles are functions, not professions and not titles.
- Each sign can be held by many people.
- Seals are granted for contribution, never sold.
- Neither chamber is subordinate to the other.

## 4. Decisions

1. Each chamber decides its internal matters by its own rules.
2. A decision that affects one chamber requires that chamber's consent.
3. A decision that affects both chambers or the whole Order requires the consent of both.
4. Treasury spending, changes to this charter, and admission rules always affect the whole Order.

## 5. The Hexastorm

- Six seats, one per sign. A Mage equals an Architect; a Trickster equals an Inquisitor.
- The Hexastorm coordinates, frames proposals and helps resolve tensions.
- It holds **no automatic power** over the chambers.
- Seat selection and terms: *to be proposed.*

## 6. Houses

Working units of the Order:

| House | Domain | Held by |
|---|---|---|
| The Inquisition | Security, investigations, forensics | Inquisitors |
| Cirqus Intelligence | Research, signals, analysis | Oracles |
| The Arsenal | Assay of tools — hallmarked, not reviewed | Curated by Alchemists, built by Architects |

## 7. Exit

Any member can leave at any time and burn their Seal. Leaving never forfeits authorship of anything they created.

## 8. Amendments

This charter is amended by pull request to this file, approved under rule 4.3.
